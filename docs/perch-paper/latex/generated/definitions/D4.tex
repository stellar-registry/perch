\begin{perchdefinition}{4}{Window, check and \emph{spent}}\label{def:window-check-and-spent}
\[\begin{aligned}&\mathit{inWindow}_P(L, t) \iff \max(0,\, L - P) < t \le L\\
&\mathit{check}_{\lambda, P}(h, \ell, x) = h' \;\land\; x > 0 \implies\\
&\qquad \sum_{k:\ \mathit{inWindow}_P(\ell,\, t_k)} y_k \;+\; x \;\le\; \lambda\\
&\qquad \land\ h' = ((t_k, y_k))_{k:\ \mathit{inWindow}_P(\ell,\, t_k)}\\
&\qquad\qquad \cdot\, (\ell, x)\end{aligned}\]
A record is a list \(h = ((t_k, y_k))_{k=1}^{m}\) of positive charges in ledger order, its total their sum. The check of a charge \(x\) at ledger \(\ell\) refuses \(x < 0\); accepts \(x = 0\), leaving \(h\); and otherwise refuses a ledger before the newest charge, an overflow, \fact{evaluator-history-bound} entries already kept, or a window total above \(\lambda\). On acceptance it keeps the entries in the window at \(\ell\) and appends \((\ell, x)\). \(\mathit{spent}_j(R, \rho, \vec a)\) lists \((\ell_a, x)\) for each positive charge that the accepted authorizations of run \(\vec a\) make to rule \(j\), in order.\leannames{\repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/a3c92ba074dcac4052727b7599e5fd8674af6c0a/formal/RuleCore/Model/Cap.lean\#L205}{Params.inWindow}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/a3c92ba074dcac4052727b7599e5fd8674af6c0a/formal/RuleCore/Model/Cap.lean\#L122}{Params.check}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/a3c92ba074dcac4052727b7599e5fd8674af6c0a/formal/RuleCore/Model/Authorize.lean\#L200}{RuleSet.spent}}
