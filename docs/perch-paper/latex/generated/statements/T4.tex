\begin{perchstatement}{4}{Attenuation narrows authority.}\label{stmt:attenuation-narrows-authority}
\[\mathit{Derives}(R, R') \implies R' \sqsubseteq R\]
Successful attenuation by the ten attenuation steps yields a rule set narrower than its parent: under any signers, every call the child allows, the parent allows. Repeated derivation preserves this relationship. \emph{allows} reads no cap or expiry, so the steps that add or tighten them narrow only over runs (Theorem~5); parent and child keep separate records, and neither theorem bounds what they spend together. The account has no entry point that installs an attenuated grant at the pinned build.
