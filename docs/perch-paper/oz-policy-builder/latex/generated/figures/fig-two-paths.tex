\caption{\textbf{One guarded call in two accounts.} Phase by phase through \code{\_\_check\_auth} for a lost guess, with notes for a guess over the cap and a direct transfer: under Perch, and under an OpenZeppelin account whose rules carry oz-policy-builder's \code{policy-\allowbreak{}interpreter} and OpenZeppelin's \code{spending\_limit}. The Perch signer is a passkey checked by a verifier contract. oz-policy-builder's rules accept only delegated signers; here the signer is a Stellar account whose own authorization entry the host checks. The OpenZeppelin account runs a rule's policies in the rule's stored order, which for policies given at creation is their address order. A cross marks where each design refuses a guess over the cap: Perch before any signer is authenticated, the OpenZeppelin account in \code{spending\_limit} after authentication. Dashed: no such phase in that design, or not reached in the scenario.
