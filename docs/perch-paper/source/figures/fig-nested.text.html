The app’s grant has two rules.

  • Rule game: any function of the game contract, signed by the app’s key.
  • Rule pay-game: the XLM token’s transfer only; argument 0 is the account itself; only when the entry’s root call is exactly game.guess; at most 2 XLM in any 100 ledgers.

The entry the app signs holds a tree of two calls, and the account checks each against its rule.

  1. game.guess(3, account), the root call: rule game accepts a call to the game contract.
  2. XLM.transfer(account, game, 1 XLM), made by the game on a loss: rule pay-game accepts it because it is from the account, the entry’s root is exactly game.guess, and it brings the window to 1 of 2 XLM.

Refused, with nothing written:

  • the same transfer signed as its own entry: its root is XLM.transfer
  • the transfer under game.other, or under another game’s guess
  • a third loss within 100 ledgers: over the cap
