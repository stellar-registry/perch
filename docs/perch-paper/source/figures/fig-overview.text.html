1. The owner installs a grant in the Perch account.
  2. The app or agent signs a call with its own key. The Soroban host asks the account to authorize it.
  3. The account's decision function checks the installed rules and spending window. A refusal stops here without storing a new record.
  4. Only after the decision accepts does the verifier contract check the signature. A failed signature also leaves the record unchanged.
  5. Only after both checks pass does the account store the new spending record.
