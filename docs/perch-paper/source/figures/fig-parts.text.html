The owner signs with a passkey in a wallet; an app signs its calls with its own grant key. When an authorization entry is used, the Soroban host calls the account’s __check_auth.

Inside the account contract (perch_account.wasm), the tested account shell runs __check_auth: its guards, the digest and the signer checks. For ordinary authorization it calls the linked rule_eval library. Its decision function and document-lowering functions are covered through their Lean translations (double border). Recovery completion follows a separate controller path. Storage holds the account's rules, installed grants, spending records, grant generation and credential index.

Calls leave the account at these times:

  • after the ordinary evaluator accepts: external signers use verifier contracts, Ed25519 or WebAuthn (trusted, hatched); delegated signers use the host’s approval path;
  • every install: the stateless document compiler, which resolves verifier names in the perch/verifiers registry (tested);
  • account-document writes, upgrades, cancellations and completions: the recovery controller (tested);
  • enrollment: the zero-knowledge pool (tested);
  • an owner-authorized execute: any contract.
