The task: An app key may send XLM from the account only to the shop, at most 10 XLM in any 100-ledger window (OpenZeppelin: per-call bound 10 XLM in the interpreter plus spending_limit 10 XLM per 100 ledgers; Perch: one grant with a 10 XLM per 100-ledger cap). Each item gives, for each design, the contracts called while the account authorized the request, in order, and where it was refused.

  1. The owner installs the rule (command 1). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (add_context_rule, __check_auth) → spending_limit (install) → policy-interpreter (install); applied. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (apply_scope, __check_auth) → Ed25519 verifier (verify) → document compiler (compile_doc) → Ed25519 verifier (batch_canonicalize_key); applied.
  2. The app pays the shop 6 XLM (command 2). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth) → spending_limit (enforce) → policy-interpreter (enforce); applied. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth) → Ed25519 verifier (verify); applied.
  3. 5 XLM more in the same window (command 3). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth) → spending_limit (enforce); refused: 3221 · SpendingLimitExceeded. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth); refused: 5 · RuleEvalRefused.
  4. 1 XLM to another recipient (command 4). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth) → spending_limit (enforce) → policy-interpreter (enforce); refused: 100 · ArgMismatch. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth); refused: 5 · RuleEvalRefused.
  5. Another key signs in the app's name (command 5). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth); refused: Error(Crypto, InvalidInput). Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth) → Ed25519 verifier (verify); refused: Error(Crypto, InvalidInput).
  6. A key not on the rule signs as itself (command 6). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth); refused: 3016 · UnauthorizedSigner. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth); refused: 5 · RuleEvalRefused.
  7. 100 ledgers later, 5 XLM (command 8). OpenZeppelin, 2 authorization entries: OpenZeppelin account (__check_auth) → spending_limit (enforce) → policy-interpreter (enforce); applied. Perch, 1 authorization entry: Perch account (__check_auth) → Ed25519 verifier (verify); applied.
