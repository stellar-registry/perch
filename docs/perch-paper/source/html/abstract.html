ABSTRACT

Perch lets the owner of a Stellar smart account give an app or agent a grant: a permission naming the contracts it may call, whom it may pay, which keys must sign, and how much it may spend in any window of ledgers. The account checks every call against the grant at one decision point and keeps the spending record in its own storage, so the limit holds over time, not only per call. An app can assign a narrower grant to a helper, and when the account is recovered, all earlier grants are revoked at once. The decision function, which runs on chain, is also translated into the Lean proof assistant, where machine-checked proofs show that it accepts only what the rules allow and keeps every window within its cap. This page also runs the same task through Perch and an OpenZeppelin smart account whose rules oz-policy-builder writes, to show where the two designs differ. Both run from their real contract code, offline in the browser, in soroban-sim, which packages the Soroban host the network runs.
