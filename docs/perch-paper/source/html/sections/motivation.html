How this fits Stellar, and the exact grant

A Stellar smart account is a contract that approves calls in its name. Soroban runs that contract. Perch packages target, function, argument predicates, signer requirements, optional expiry and a spending cap into a policy document. Other designs offer bounded delegation too; Perch's focus is a single account-owned decision with an inspectable document. {{cite:stellar-custom-account,metamask-delegation,oz-policy-builder}}
