{{badge:plain:PREVIEW}} Try it. Pick one of the café's three orders: one within the grant's cap, one over it, and one from a tampered checkout. The dialog opens, simulates the order, and lets you approve or reject it.

The dialog is the real <soroban-sign-dialog> component from soroban-sim, styled to look like the Nido wallet. So that the demo runs offline, its simulation results are recorded rather than computed live: they come from a soroban-sim run of the paper's Perch contracts (what is real and what is recorded). The café has its own grant, separate from the shop grant: 10 XLM per 100 ledgers, with 3 XLM already spent. Unlike the shop grant, it doesn't limit whom the account can pay, which is why the tampered checkout's extra transfer could go through.
