ABSTRACT

The main Perch paper describes one grant and how the account decides it. This companion describes the rest of the design at the pinned build: the full account, with its compiler, verifiers and registry; how a grant is installed and stored; every check the account runs before it decides; recovery under two threat profiles, with guardians, a zero-knowledge factor and a grant generation that stops every earlier grant from authorizing through the account; and the adversaries beyond the grantee. It also describes proposed work: an agent runtime with delegation-tree budgets, payments over x402, Cedar as an authoring language, and checking the proofs in the page. It carries the earlier live experiment, which runs Perch's account, compiler, verifier and recovery controller offline in the browser. Everything described as proposed is not built.
