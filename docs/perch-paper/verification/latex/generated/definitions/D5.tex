\begin{perchdefinition}{5}{Derivation}\label{def:derivation}
\[\begin{aligned}&\mathit{Derives}(R, R)\\
&\mathit{Derives}(R, R') \land \mathit{attenuate}(R', \sigma) = R''\\
&\qquad \implies \mathit{Derives}(R, R'')\end{aligned}\]
\(\sigma\) is one of rule-core's ten attenuation steps: drop a rule; remove functions, or restrict functions; add an argument condition; remove signers, or raise a threshold; add a cap, lower its limit, or lengthen its period; set or lower the last ledger.
