\begin{perchdefinition}{6}{Documents and lowering}\label{def:documents-and-lowering}
\[X = \mathit{excluded}(d_s, A)\]
For a document \(d\) on account \(A\), \(\mathit{Doc.allows}(d, A, c, S)\) holds when some rule of \(d\) covers \(c\)'s contract and function, its argument constraints hold and its principals are signed by \(S\); no rule allows a creation. \(\mathit{Doc.Valid}(d)\) holds when every rule that requires all its signers names distinct signers, fewer than \(2^{32}\) of them, and no rule has a not-after ledger of 0. \(\mathit{lower}(d, A)\) is the rules of an account document, followed by the recovery rule when \(d\) configures recovery. With \(X = \mathit{excluded}(d_s, A)\) for the account document \(d_s\), which is \(A\), the adopted controller and, if configured, the zero-knowledge adapter and pool, \(\mathit{lowerScoped}(d, A, X, g)\) refuses a document that configures recovery or has a rule whose target is \(A\), a member of \(X\) or the account's self-administration, and otherwise lowers \(d\)'s rules stamped with generation \(g\).
