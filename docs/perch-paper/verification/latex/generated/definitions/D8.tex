\begin{perchdefinition}{8}{Host run}\label{def:host-run}
A model of soroban-env-host running a transaction of contract calls under enforcing authorization. Every call is a frame that a failure rolls back. \code{require\_auth} of an address holds when the frame's direct caller is that address (invoker authority), or when an authorization entry of the address matches; on its first match the address's \code{\_\_check\_auth} runs on the entry's contexts in preorder, then the expiration is checked and the nonce consumed. Stellar-account signers, \code{require\_auth} inside \code{\_\_check\_auth}, \code{require\_\allowbreak{}auth\_\allowbreak{}for\_\allowbreak{}args}, \code{authorize\_\allowbreak{}as\_\allowbreak{}current\_\allowbreak{}contract}, contract creation as a call and source-account credentials are outside the model.\leannames{\repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Soroban/Host.lean\#L342}{Soroban.Tx.run}}
\end{perchdefinition}
