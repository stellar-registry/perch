\begin{perchdefinition}{9}{Denotation}\label{def:denotation}
\[\forall x.\ \exists y.\ f_{\mathrm{T}}(x) = \mathsf{ok}(y) \land \llbracket y \rrbracket = f(\llbracket x \rrbracket)\]
\(\llbracket \cdot \rrbracket\) maps the translation's values (byte vectors, \code{u32} and \code{u64} scalars, rules, caps, records, contexts, signers and outcomes) to the model's. A translated function \(f_{\mathrm{T}}\) agrees with its model \(f\) when, for every input, it succeeds and its result denotes the model's result on the denoted input. Theorem~\ref{stmt:total-equivalence-of-the-evaluator} and Theorem~\ref{stmt:lowering-equivalence} are stated this way.\leannames{\repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Equiv/Denote.lean\#L104}{Equiv.denoteRules}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Equiv/Authorize.lean\#L60}{denoteAuthorized}}
