\begin{perchstatement}{3}{Signer accounting.}\label{stmt:signer-accounting}
\[\begin{aligned}&\mathit{checkAuth}(R, \rho, (\vec c, \vec j, S, \ell, g)) = \rho'\\
&\qquad \implies \forall s \in S.\ \exists i.\ s \in \Sigma_{R_{j_i}}\\
&\mathit{signed}(r, S) \iff k_r \le |\Sigma_r \cap S|\\
&S \subseteq S' \land \mathit{allows}(R, c, S) \implies \mathit{allows}(R, c, S')\end{aligned}\]
Acceptance implies every supplied signer occurs in a chosen rule, and a threshold counts distinct listed identities. Adding identities preserves a rule's permission, though not whole-batch acceptance if an added identity is in no chosen rule. Distinct identities need not represent independent keys or controlling parties: an external identity includes its verifier address as well as its key.

\begin{perchstatement}{7}{Delegation-tree budgets.}\label{stmt:delegation-tree-budgets}
\[\begin{aligned}&\mathit{valid}(T) \land (\forall n \in T.\ \mathit{spent}_n = 0)\\
&\quad \land\ \mathit{run}(T, \vec o) = (T', \mathit{log}) \land T'_a = n\\
&\qquad \implies \mathit{spentUnder}(T', \mathit{log}, a) \le \mathit{allowance}_n\end{aligned}\]
\(\mathit{valid}(T)\) says that every node's parent index is below its own and no allowance or spent amount is negative; \(\mathit{run}\) applies spends, delegations and refills in order, keeping the tree on a refused one and logging each successful spend; \(\mathit{spentUnder}(T', \mathit{log}, a)\) totals the logged spends made at or under node \(a\), and \(\mathit{allowance}_n\) is that node's allowance at the end. From a valid tree with nothing spent, spending beneath every node is at most its allowance, including when its children's allowances add up to more than its own. Allowances are lifetime totals, not windows, and refills raise them.

\begin{perchstatement}{13}{Canonical identity.}\label{stmt:canonical-identity}
\[\mathit{emitDoc}(d_1) = \mathit{emitDoc}(d_2) \implies d_1 = d_2\]
