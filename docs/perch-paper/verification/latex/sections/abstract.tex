\begin{abstract}
This companion sets out what is proved about Perch, and about which object.
Perch's authorization decision is a Rust function in the rule-core library,
written in a subset that Charon and Aeneas translate into Lean. We give the
definitions and all thirteen statements: the translation's equivalence with a
readable model; acceptance implies permission; signer accounting; attenuation
and trace inclusion; cap-window safety; delegation-tree budgets; grant
containment and the containment chain; recovery invalidation in an account
model; a host model of Soroban authorization; lowering equivalence; and
canonical identity. Each comes with its hypotheses and its Lean names at a fixed
revision. We then list the obligations on the account code around the evaluator
that carry the theorems to the deployed account, the argued proposition they support, what the proofs trust,
and what remains unproved.
\keywords{Formal verification \and Lean \and Aeneas \and Authorization \and
Smart accounts \and Soroban}
\end{abstract}
