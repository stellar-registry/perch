Perch

Safe, bounded delegation for apps and agents on Stellar: the paper, its companions, and a simulation that runs Perch and OpenZeppelin side by side.

  • Perch paper — bounded grants for apps and agents on Stellar: what a grant can say, how the account decides, what is proved, and both designs run side by side. The academic edition (PDF) is built from the same source.
  • Simulation — an OpenZeppelin account with oz-policy-builder's policy and a Perch account, run side by side in soroban-sim in the browser, offline.
  • Companion papers:
    • Perch and oz-policy-builder — the full comparison, where each design does better, and lessons for Perch (PDF).
    • What Perch proves — every theorem and definition, the translated evaluator and its models (PDF).
    • The Perch vision — recovery, the full account, and proposed agent and Cedar work, with the recovery experiment.
  • Walkthrough — one payment, step by step, under both designs.
  • Slide deck — an introduction to an earlier Perch design, which compiled documents into OpenZeppelin context rules and policies.
  • Cedar walkthrough — an interactive guide to Cedar, with an official-engine lab and a separate Perch compiler prototype.

The experiments describe their source revisions and limitations. Proposed extensions are not deployed features.
