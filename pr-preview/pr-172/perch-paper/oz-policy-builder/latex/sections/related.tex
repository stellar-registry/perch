Composition is common on EVM chains: ERC-7579~\cite{erc7579} accounts install
validators and hooks as modules, Smart Sessions (Rhinestone and Biconomy)
restricts a session key through policies~\cite{smart-sessions}, and MetaMask's
delegation framework restricts a delegation through caveat
enforcers~\cite{metamask-delegation}. Synthesis from recordings relates to
policy mining from logs~\cite{xu-stoller-mining}.
