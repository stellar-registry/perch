The decision function is about {{fact:rule-eval-lines}} lines of Rust. The Charon {{cite:charon}} and Aeneas {{cite:aeneas}} tools translate it into Lean {{cite:lean4}}, a proof assistant whose kernel checks every proof, and theorems relate the translation to a readable model written by hand. rule-core's CI regenerates the translation and fails on any difference, and Perch's tests run {{fact:lean-doc-traces}} traces generated from the Lean model through the compiled account, checking every verdict and spending record against the model.

  • The translation behaves as the model does. On every input, the Lean translation of the Rust returns exactly what the model returns, without panicking or looping. If the model refuses the shop grant's second payment of {{fact:sim-second-pay-xlm}} XLM, so does the translated code.
  • An accepted call is always allowed by a rule. If the decision function accepts a request, every call in it is allowed by a rule it was given, under the signers it was given. Under the shop grant, every accepted call is a transfer from the account to the shop that the app's key signed.
  • No window holds more than the cap. However the app times its payments, no window of {{fact:shop-period-ledgers}} ledgers holds more than {{fact:shop-cap-xlm}} XLM of accepted charges. {{ref:fig:fig-cap-window}} is one instance: 6 and 4 XLM fill the window, 1 XLM at ledger t + 99 is refused, and 5 XLM at t + 100 is accepted because the 6 XLM charged at t has left the window.
  • A narrowed grant never allows more. A grant derived by attenuation allows no call its parent does not, under any signers. A helper narrowed from the shop grant can pay no one but the shop.
  • No grant reaches the account itself. No installed grant, and no grant derived from one, allows a call to the account or to the recovery contracts it has adopted. The account refuses at install any rule that targets it, so an app cannot use its grant to install a wider one.
  • Recovery revokes every earlier grant. After a completed recovery, no earlier grant, and no grant derived from one, accepts a request with a call in it. An app that held the shop grant before the recovery can make no further payment through it.

The formal version. What Perch proves states all {{fact:statements-count}} theorems with their definitions, hypotheses and Lean names, among them the six above (Theorems 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10), and sets out what each one assumes. The PDF edition gives the core results in mathematical form.
