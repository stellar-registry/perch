Twelve dimensions, side by side, read from source at the pins.

{{ref:tab:tab-compare}} sets the two designs side by side on twelve of the twenty dimensions. Its cells are short. {{ref:tab:tab-compare-full}} in {{ref:sec:sources}} adds eight more dimensions (rules with no signer, rules shared across accounts, account-key recovery, install ordering, authoring tools, who can change deployed code, keeping state alive, and what a refusal reports), a source for every cell, and a mark: V for checked in source by the authors at the pin, R for cited from source by an internal reader and not re-read by the authors, U for not verified.

Two rows need a word. What a signature binds differs only for delegated signers, where OpenZeppelin binds the selected rule IDs and Perch does not; for external signers both bind the account and the rule IDs. Assurance compares different kinds of evidence about different objects: proofs about a translation of Perch's decision function, and tests of oz-policy-builder's shipped contracts. Neither ranks above the other, and neither is an audit.
