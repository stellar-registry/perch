A per-call bound alone

The interpreter keeps no totals (docs/stellar.md:404-407). We ran that configuration once, outside the walkthrough: on {{fact:opb-local-network}}, an OpenZeppelin account whose rule carried only the interpreter, with a per-call bound of {{fact:opb-per-call-bound-xlm}} XLM, applied {{fact:opb-per-call-applied-sends}} sends that moved {{fact:opb-per-call-moved-xlm}} XLM, and refused one of {{fact:opb-per-call-bound-xlm}} XLM and a stroop. The same point, recorded and rerunnable

The dual simulation in the Perch paper runs the shop task with the real contract code of both designs in one host, offline; its native, WASI and browser runs give the same replies, wall-clock timings aside. The {{fact:sim-pay-xlm}}-XLM payment applies in both designs, and the {{fact:sim-second-pay-xlm}} XLM after it is refused in both: by Perch in its decision, with no verifier call, and by the OpenZeppelin account in spending_limit ({{fact:oz-spending-limit-exceeded}}). The owner then removes spending_limit, and the interpreter's per-call bound of {{fact:sim-oz-per-call-bound-xlm}} XLM applies {{fact:sim-oz-percall-sends}} {{fact:sim-pay-xlm}}-XLM sends ({{fact:sim-oz-percall-moved-xlm}} XLM). Perch refuses all three, because its window already holds {{fact:sim-pay-xlm}}. Re-adding spending_limit starts an empty history, as replacing a Perch rule starts an empty window.
