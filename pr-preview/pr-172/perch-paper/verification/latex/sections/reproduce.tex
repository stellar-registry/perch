The first line builds the model, the translation and every theorem, with its
axiom record; the second fails if the committed translation differs from a fresh
extraction (it needs Nix, or the \code{nixos/nix} image); the third checks the
claims ledger; the fourth regenerates the conformance vectors and traces from the
model.
